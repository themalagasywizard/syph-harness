---
name: summarise
description: Summarise a document, page or paper into a short brief with the key points first.
---
1. Read the whole source before writing. If it is a URL, fetch it with web_fetch.
2. Open with one sentence that states the main point.
3. Follow with at most 5 bullets, most important first, each under 25 words.
4. Keep numbers, names and dates exactly as in the source.
5. End with the source title or link on its own line.
